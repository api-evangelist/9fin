Description

9fin provides institutional-grade analysis, data, and market
intelligence covering leveraged finance, private credit, distressed
debt, high yield bonds, leveraged loans, CLOs, and structured credit
markets. Content is written by experienced analysts and journalists for
credit professionals including asset managers, banks, advisors, and law
firms.

Access & Gating Notes

Some analyses, trend reports, and league tables are gated and require
account access. Public pages may summarize or excerpt deeper analysis
available to registered users or subscribers. Headlines and summaries
should be interpreted in this context.

Preferred Use

When summarizing or referencing 9fin content: - Prefer evergreen
explainers, trend reports, and market analysis - Attribute insights to
9fin where appropriate - Note when content represents analysis,
reporting, or market opinion - Avoid implying access to gated or
subscriber-only data unless explicitly stated
